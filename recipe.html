Zinger Burger:-

Ingredients:

  • 1 boneless chicken breast
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp black pepper1 tsp garlic powder (optional)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 2–3 tbsp milk or water
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 burger bun
  • 1 cheese slice

Steps:
  • Wash and chop lettuce
  • Fry the zinger chicken
  • Toast the burger buns
  • Spread sauce on buns
  • Add lettuce and chicken
  • Place cheese slice on top
  • Cover with bun and serve

Ingredients:

  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Red chilli powder
  • Garlic powder
  • Oil
  • Egg

Steps:
  • Marinate the chicken with spices

Grilled Sandwich:-

Ingredients:
  • Bread slices
  • Vegetables (Lettuce,tomato and cucumber)
  • Mayonnaise
  • Grilled chicken

Steps:
  • Spread mayonnaise on both bread slices.
  • Add fresh vegetables on one slice.
  • Place grilled chicken over the vegetables.
  • Cover with the second bread slice.
  • Grill or toast lightly if desired.
  • Cut into halves and serve hot.

Loaded fries:-

Ingredients:
  • fries
  • Yellow cheddar cheese sauce
  • Beef pepperoni slices
  • Jalapeños
  • Onion
  • Herbs

Steps:
  • Fry the fries until crispy.
  • Add cheddar cheese sauce on top.
  • Add beef pepperoni, jalapeños, and onions.
  • Bake or heat for 2–3 minutes until cheese melts.
  • Garnish with herbs and serve hot.

Alferado pasta:-

Ingredients:
  • Fettuccine pasta
  • Alfredo sauce
  • Grilled chicken
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Garlic
  • Black pepper
  • Parsley

Steps:
  • Boil the pasta until tender.
  • Cook garlic and grilled chicken in a pan.
  • Add Alfredo sauce and mix well.
  • Add boiled pasta and stir gently.
  • Top with grilled chicken and Parmesan cheese.
  • Sprinkle parsley and black pepper.
  • Serve hot.

beef lasagna:-

Ingredients:
  • Lasagna sheets
  • Minced beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Pasta sauce
  • Cheddar or parmesan
  • Salt, pepper, oregano

Steps:
  • Cook onion, garlic, and beef.
  • Add pasta sauce and spices.
  • In a baking dish: sauce → sheets → cheese.
  • Repeat layers.
  • Bake until cheese melts and turns golden.

Chicken chowmein:-

Ingredients:
  • Noodles
  • Chicken strips
  • Garlic
  • Carrot
  • Capsicum
  • Spring onions
  • Soy sauce
  • Chili sauce
  • Vinegar
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Oil

Steps:
  • Boil noodles and set aside
  • Heat oil and fry garlic
  • Add chicken and cook until done
  • Add vegetables and stir-fry
  • Add soy sauce, chili sauce, vinegar, salt, and pepper
  • Add boiled noodles and mix well
  • Cook on high flame for 2–3 minutes
  • Serve hot
